# Acta design: chat, settings, data safety and updates

Part of the Acta design system. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) is the index: direction, principles, the never-list and the review checklist. Section numbers are shared across all parts.

## 5. Components (continued): chat, settings, data safety and updates

States, the focus ring and the controls used here (buttons, inputs, rows, menus, dialog, notice, empty state) are in [components.md](components.md).

**Chat panel** (a tool window; ADR 0009; Windows only for now: macOS has the link, like Linux, until the web view can tell the page's own navigations from its frames'). It shows claude.ai, the website, in the system web view. Acta never reads from the page, writes to it or styles it, so the page keeps claude.ai's own theme whatever Acta's is.
- **Header** (4.5): name "Chat", context "claude.ai" (plain text; no logo of Anthropic or Claude anywhere). Buttons: Open in browser (`external-link`), Reload (`rotate-cw`), `ellipsis` (Sign-in help, Move to bottom / Move to right, Sign out and clear chat data...), hide. Tooltips of these buttons always open upward, away from the web view, which would cover them.
- **Toggle**: `Mod+Shift+H`, the titlebar toggle, palette "Toggle chat". `Mod+4` focuses it. Showing the panel focuses the page inside it; nothing else ever gives it focus.
- **Placement**: the right dock by default, sharing it with the terminal one at a time (4.5); move either to the bottom dock to see both.
- **States.** All but the live one are drawn by Acta on `bg.sunken` as an empty state at the top left of the panel (padding 16, text at most 480 wide).
  - *Not started* (the web view is created only when the user asks). Heading "Chat with Claude beside your work". Sentence: "This panel shows claude.ai, the website, in your system's web view. You sign in with your own account, and Acta does not read or change anything on the page." Then, in `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "Once started, the web view uses about 400 MB of memory, also while the panel is hidden." Buttons: "Open claude.ai here" (primary), "Open in your browser" (ghost).
  - *Starting*: the empty surface; after 400 ms one line, "Opening claude.ai...", in `type.small` `text.tertiary`.
  - *Live*: the web view fills the panel under the header. Acta draws nothing over it.
  - *Sign-in help*: a Notice at the top of the panel, above the web view, the first time the panel is live and whenever "Sign-in help" is chosen: `info`, "Sign in with email: the link in the message opens in your browser and shows a code; type that code here. Signing in with Google or Apple may be refused in this panel.", action "Got it".
  - *Under an overlay.* The web view is a native view above everything Acta draws, so while the palette, quick capture, a dialog, a menu or the sidebar overlay is open it is hidden. Its place shows `bg.sunken` and one line at the top left, "The chat is hidden while this is open.", in `type.small` `text.tertiary`. It returns at the same bounds, without animation, when the overlay closes. Toasts and tooltips are placed so they never reach into the panel.
  - *Web view missing* (Windows without the WebView2 runtime). Heading "The chat panel needs WebView2". Sentence: "Microsoft's WebView2 runtime is not installed on this computer, so Acta cannot show claude.ai here. Everything else in Acta works." Buttons: "Open claude.ai in your browser" (primary), "Get WebView2" (ghost; Microsoft's download page in the system browser), "Try again" (ghost).
  - *Could not open*: heading "claude.ai could not be opened here", the reason when known ("There is no connection."), buttons "Try again" (primary) and "Open claude.ai in your browser" (ghost). The panel never fails to a blank surface.
- **Sign out and clear chat data...** is a confirmed case of 6.4, because the profile cannot be brought back: "Sign out and clear chat data?", "This deletes the panel's cookies and site data from this computer. Your conversations stay in your Claude account.", Cancel, "Sign out and clear". Afterwards the panel is back to *Not started*.
- **Keyboard.** While the page has focus, keys belong to the page, `Mod+K` included. The short list Acta keeps, per platform, is in 6.1; the palette is reached with `Mod+Shift+K`.
- **macOS and Linux** (macOS for now, ADR 0009 changelog 2026-11-21): there is no panel and no titlebar toggle. "Open claude.ai in your browser" is a palette command and a row in Settings, and `Mod+Shift+H` runs it.

**Settings page** (a page like any other: `Mod+,`, the Settings row of the sidebar, or the palette; it opens in the active tab). Title "Settings". Sections are headed in `type.subtitle`, 32 apart. A **setting row** is at least 44 high with 8 of vertical padding: at the left the label in `type.ui` `text.primary` and under it a description in `type.small` `text.tertiary` (at most 420 wide, wraps); at the right the control. No boxes, no lines. Every setting applies the moment it changes, has no Save button, and exists as a palette command.

| Section | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Appearance** | Theme: segmented control System / Light / Dark; description "System follows your computer's setting." Language: dropdown English / Français; description "Changes Acta's own text. What you and Claude Code write is never translated." Applies at once, without a restart. Terminal text size: a stepper 11 to 18. Also "Increase terminal text size" and "Decrease terminal text size" in the palette, and `Ctrl+Alt+=` / `Ctrl+Alt+-` on Windows and Linux, `Cmd+Shift+=` / `Cmd+Shift+-` on macOS. Never `Ctrl+-` or `Ctrl+Shift+-`: both reach Claude Code as its undo. Nothing else in 6.1 uses `Ctrl+Alt`; on a layout where AltGr with one of these keys types a character, the character wins and the palette is the way. |

**Keyboard shortcuts page** (`Mod+/`, the palette, or About in Settings; a page with the fixed key `shortcuts`). Title "Keyboard shortcuts"; a search input ("Filter shortcuts", focused on open) that filters as typed; then the shortcuts of 6.1 grouped under `type.label` headings (General, Tabs and tool windows, Lists and tree, Editors, Working folders, Terminal and chat). Rows of 28: the action in `type.ui` `text.primary`, its keys as kbd hints at the right; no lines. It shows the bindings of the platform it runs on, with the key names of the interface language, and it is read-only: there is no rebinding in v1. Until tabs, pane cycling and zooming a tool window exist, `Ctrl+Tab`, `Ctrl+Shift+Tab`, `F6`, `Shift+F6` and `Mod+Shift+Enter` are listed as reserved: Acta already keeps them from the chat's page and the terminal, and they do nothing yet. No match: "No shortcut matches "{query}"."

**Export dialog** (palette: "Export...", also in settings). Dialog of 440. Title "Export". Scope: segmented control (Everything / This project). Then a notice with `triangle-alert` in `warning`: "The file is not encrypted. Anyone who can open it can read these projects. That is what lets you read it without Acta or its key." Then a checkbox "Protect with a passphrase" (not in v1: format 1 of the export has no encryption, so the dialog shows no checkbox until a format carries one); when checked, two inputs (passphrase, repeat) and the line "Acta cannot recover a lost passphrase." in `type.small` `text.secondary`. Buttons: Cancel, "Choose location..." (primary; opens the system save dialog, default name `acta-2026-11-20.acta.json`). Result: toast "Exported 4 projects to acta-2026-11-20.acta.json. Show in folder". The same one-line warning appears on "Save document as file". Settings shows "Last export: 12 days ago" in `type.small`; once, after the tenth topic exists, a notice at the top of the active page suggests a first export ("Everything is stored on this computer only. An export is your copy if the system keychain is ever lost.", actions Export..., Dismiss) and never returns.

**Import** (palette: "Import..."): system open dialog, then, if the file is protected, a passphrase input; then the **import preview**, a dialog of 520. Title: Import "acta-2026-11-20.acta.json". Meta line in `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "Exported 20 Nov 2026 · format 1 · 4 projects". A table (see 8.8): one row per kind (Projects, Epics, Topics, Entries, Notes, Documents, Memories), three `tnum` columns Created / Updated / Skipped, zeros in `text.tertiary`. Below: "Nothing here will be deleted. Skipped records are the same as, or older than, what Acta already has." and, when Updated is not zero, a ghost "Show what changes" expanding the titles of updated records (max 240 high, scrolls). Buttons: Cancel (focused), Import. Nothing is written before Import; Acta takes a snapshot first, so the result line reads "Imported: 12 created, 3 updated. A snapshot from before the import is kept." Working folders in the file belong to the computer that exported it; those that do not exist here are kept and add one sentence to the result: "3 working folders were not found on this computer. Locate each one from its project, epic or topic." Refusals replace the table with one notice and a single Close button: "This file was made by a newer Acta (format 3). Update Acta, then import it." / "This is not an Acta export." / "Wrong passphrase."

**Trash page** (palette: "Open trash"; a page). Title "Trash", line "Deleted things stay here for 30 days." in `type.small` `text.tertiary`. Rows of 32 grouped by day of deletion (group headers `type.small` `text.tertiary`): kind icon 16 (`folder`, `layers`, status glyph, `message-square`, `sticky-note`, `file-text`, `pin`), title `type.ui`, original place in `type.small` `text.tertiary`, "by Claude Code" or the name, days left ("28 days left", `tnum`). `Enter` or `R` restores to the original place and shows "Topic restored. Undo"; if its epic is also in the trash, both come back and the toast says so. A project row counts its content ("31 topics, 12 notes"). No "empty trash" and no "delete now" in v1: the trash exists so that nothing is ever lost by a key press, and a purge button is exactly such a key press. Empty: "Trash is empty."

**Snapshot restore** (palette: "Restore from snapshot..."): dialog of 520, titled "Restore from snapshot", listing snapshots, newest first, rows of 32: date and time (`tnum`), kind ("Daily", "Weekly", "Before update to 0.4.1", "Before import", "Before restore"), size. Selecting one enables the destructive-styled button "Restore and restart" and shows: "Acta will restart with its data as of 18 Nov 2026, 09:12. Everything as it is now is saved as a snapshot first, so you can come back." This is one of the confirmed cases in 6.4. After restart a notice reads "Restored the snapshot from 18 Nov 2026, 09:12." If a migration fails after an update, Acta restores by itself and the next launch shows the notice "The update to 0.4.1 could not convert the database. Acta put back the snapshot from just before; nothing was lost." with the action "Details".

**Key-loss recovery screen** (replaces the whole window content, like first run; see 8.9). Shown when the database file exists but its key is not in the system keychain. Calm and exact: no red, no icon larger than 16, no "error" in the heading. It states what is true (the file is intact, unreadable, untouched), why it usually happens, and offers three actions in order of safety: "Import an export..." (primary), "Try again" (secondary; re-reads the keychain, for a locked or slow keychain), "Start empty" (ghost; the unreadable file is renamed and kept, the screen says its new name). Acta never creates a database over the old one and never deletes the kept file.

**Updater window** (ADR 0004). An update is normally applied at launch, before the main window: a feed check of up to 2 s, a download of up to 5 s, then a relaunch. So that the user never stares at nothing, if the main window is not ready 300 ms after launch a small window appears at once and closes in the frame the main window opens. 360 x 132, centred on the primary display, not resizable, no caption buttons, draggable anywhere, `bg.base` with a 1px `border.default` outline (`radius.lg` where the platform allows a shaped window), padding 24, in the theme and language of the preferences file. Top to bottom, left-aligned: the app mark 24 and "Acta" in `type.ui-strong`, gap 8; 16; one status line in `type.ui` `text.primary`; 12; a progress bar (full width, 4 high, `radius.sm`, track `fill.selected`, fill `text.secondary`) with the percentage in `type.small` `text.tertiary` `tnum` right-aligned under it. The status line reads, in order, "Checking for updates..." (no bar: nothing is measurable yet, and an indeterminate bar would be animation for its own sake), "Downloading update..." (bar and percentage, updated as data arrives, never eased), "Restarting..." (bar full). There is no button and nothing to cancel: every step is bounded by ADR 0004, and when a step fails or runs out of time the window closes and Acta opens as it is, with no message. It is the one place the app mark is shown.
