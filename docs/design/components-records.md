# Acta design: record page parts

Part of the Acta design system. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) is the index: direction, principles, the never-list and the review checklist. Section numbers are shared across all parts.

## 5. Components (continued): record page parts

States, the focus ring and the controls used here (buttons, inputs, rows, menus, dialog, notice, empty state) are in [components.md](components.md).

**Timeline entry**: a two-column block, gutter 20 + gap 12, then content.
- Gutter: the author mark, 20 x 20, top-aligned with the header line. **Human**: circle, `fill.selected`, initial in 12px weight 600 `text.primary` ("A" for Alexandre). **Claude Code**: square with `radius.xs`, no fill, 1px `border.strong`, Lucide `chevron-right` 12 in `text.secondary` (a prompt mark).
- Header (line 20): author name, then entry type, then time. Human name in `type.ui-strong`; "Claude Code" in `type.code` `text.primary`. Circle plus sans versus square plus mono is the at-a-glance distinction: shape and typeface, no colour, no badge. Type: icon 14 plus word in `type.small` `text.secondary`. Time: `type.small` `text.tertiary`, `tnum`, in the long form of 10.2 ("2 h ago", "Yesterday 18:42", "Tue 14:05", "12 Nov 2026"); tooltip gives the full local date and time.
- Body: rendered Markdown, 4 below the header; the entry's files, when it has any, in the attachment strip 8 below the body (components-attachments.md 5).
- By type: **Progress** (`git-commit-horizontal`): plain. **Decision** (`signpost`): body and header sit in a 1px `border.default` box, `radius.lg`, padding 12 x 16, hanging 17 into the column gutter on each side so its author mark and text stay in the columns of the other entries, and the word "Decision" is `text.primary` weight 600; decisions are the entries people come back for, so they are the only ones with an outline. **Discussion** (`message-square`): plain.
- **System event** (status change, creation, moved): one line, no author mark, a status glyph 14 in the gutter, `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "Alexandre set status to Blocked - 2 h ago". Consecutive events within 5 minutes collapse into one line.
- States: hover reveals a ghost `ellipsis` button at the top right (also `Shift+F10` / context-menu key on the focused entry) with Edit, Copy as Markdown, Change type, Delete; focused: ring around the entry; editing: body becomes the multi-line editor in place; newly arrived from another process: `accent.subtle` background fading out (see 6.5).

**Current-state panel**: `bg.raised`, 1px `border.subtle`, `radius.lg`, padding 16. Label row: "Current state" in `type.label` `text.secondary`, right-aligned meta "Updated 2 h ago by Claude Code" in `type.small` `text.tertiary`. Body `type.reading`, intended to stay under about 6 lines; over 12 lines it clamps with a "Show all" ghost button. Empty: placeholder text "Where does this stand? One or two sentences." in `text.tertiary`.

**Note card**: `bg.raised`, 1px `border.subtle`, `radius.lg`, padding 12 x 16. Body `type.reading`, clamped to 6 lines when not focused. Meta row (`type.small` `text.tertiary`): time, then "Report to Marie" when the note names a person (components-questions.md 5; the name a link to the person page, kept after filing), and for filed notes "Filed as" plus a link to the topic or epic (id and title, `accent`), or "Dismissed"; "by Claude Code" when Claude Code filed it. Files attached to the note show in the attachment strip under the body, 8 below it and above the meta row, and stay visible when the body is clamped (components-attachments.md 5); a file dropped on the card attaches to it. Hover or focus reveals ghost icon buttons: Make topic (`corner-down-right`), Edit (`pencil`), Delete (`trash-2`); on a filed note, Back to inbox (`inbox`) instead of Make topic. Focused: focus ring. Filed: body in `text.secondary`.

**Properties block** (under the title of every record page; the model's own fields, shown as a page's typed properties). Rows of 28, two columns, no box and no dividers; 12 under the title, 24 above the next section. Label: `type.ui` `text.tertiary`, in a column exactly as wide as the longest label of the block in the current language (never a fixed width; capped at 40% of the column, beyond which a label truncates with a tooltip). Gap 16. Value: `type.ui` `text.primary`. A value that can be changed is a ghost control (padding 0 x 8, shifted left by 8 so its text aligns with plain values; hover `fill.hover`); a value that cannot is plain text. The block is one stop when stepping through a page with `Up` / `Down`; `Enter` goes inside, `Up` / `Down` move between rows, `Enter` acts on the value, `Esc` leaves. The direct keys (`S` status, `D` due date, `R` report to, `Q` new question, `M` move) act on the page's record from anywhere on the page, unless the focused element has its own meaning for the key: the focused element always wins. `M` on a focused knowledge row moves that entry, not the topic; on a folder row it does nothing; in a text field it types.

| Page | Rows, in order |
|---|---|
| Topic | **Status**: the status indicator button. **Due**: the due date (below). **Report to**: who the work is reported back to, or "None" (components-questions.md 5, Report to row). **Epic**: the epic's name, a link; `M` or the row's menu moves the topic; "None" in `text.tertiary` for a topic outside any epic. **Folders**: the working folders row. **Id**: "T-42" in `type.code` `text.secondary`, the short id Claude Code uses; activating it copies it and the value reads "Copied" for 1.5 s. **Updated**: "2 h ago by Claude Code" (time rules of the timeline; tooltip with the full date). |
| Epic | **Progress**: "3 of 7 topics done" with a bar (120 x 4, `radius.xs`, track `fill.selected`, fill `text.secondary`): topics Done over all topics not Dropped. **Due**. **Report to**. **Folders**. **Id**: "E-7". **Updated**. |
| Project | **Progress** (same rule over the whole project). **Due**. **Folders**. **Updated**. |
| Document or memory | **Kind**: "Document" or "Memory" (menu: Change kind). **Level**: the owner, "Topic Embed the terminal", a link (`M` moves the entry). **Id**: "K-7". **Updated**: "2 h ago by Claude Code". |

There are no user-defined properties and no empty rows to hide: every row is a field the model has for every record of that kind, so the agent and the human always see the same set. The due date and Report to are the optional values (added by the user's decisions of 2026-11-21; Report to on topics and epics only, never on projects): their rows are always shown, set or not, so the set of rows never changes. The direct keys gain `R` (Report to), with the same rule: the focused element wins.

**Due row** (topic, epic, project; one optional calendar date per record, day precision, no time, no recurrence; data.md 11, request 16). The value is a ghost control. Empty: "None" (`due.none`; French "Aucune", agreeing with *échéance*) in `text.tertiary`. Set: two runs 8 apart, never joined into one string: the absolute date in the `{date}` form of 10.2 ("15 Dec 2026", `text.primary`), then the relative phrase of 10.2.1, `type.ui`:

| Case (today is the local date, 10.2.1) | Relative phrase | Colour of the phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Before today, on an open record (**overdue**) | "Overdue by 3 days" (`due.overdue`) | `warning` |
| Today | "Today" (`due.today`) | `text.secondary` |
| Tomorrow | "Tomorrow" (`due.tomorrow`) | `text.secondary` |
| 2 or 3 days ahead (**due soon**, with today and tomorrow) | "In 3 days" (`due.in_days`) | `text.secondary` |
| 4 days ahead or later | "In 24 days" (`due.in_days`) | `text.tertiary` |
| Any date, on a closed record | none: the date alone, in `text.secondary` | |

**Open and closed for due dates.** A topic is open when Draft, Planned, Active or Blocked. An epic or a project is open while its Progress is below 100% or it has no topic at all. A closed record is never overdue, anywhere (page, table, tree, agent output): "Done" answers the date. Thresholds are in whole local days: overdue below 0, due soon 0 to 3, later 4 and more. The words carry the state, so the row reads in greyscale; `warning` is the colour of "needs you", the meaning Blocked already has (2.3). Measured on `bg.base`, dark / light: `warning` 6.96 / 5.87, `text.secondary` 7.50 / 7.26, `text.tertiary` 5.78 / 5.67.

Keys: `Enter` on the row, `D` from anywhere on the page, or a click opens the **due date picker** anchored to the value; `Delete` or `Backspace` on the focused row clears the date. An epic or a project shows its own date only, never one derived from its topics: the topic table's Overdue filter (below) answers "which topics are late", and a derived date would change under the reader each time a topic moved. There is no check that a topic's date falls before its epic's: dates are the human's plan, not constraints.

**Due date picker** (the one date control; opened from the Due row, from `D` on a focused tree or table row, and from the palette command "Set due date..."). A floating layer: `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.lg`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 12, width 248. It opens 4 below its anchor (the Due value; on a tree or table row, the row's title), left edges aligned, and flips above when there is less room below. From the palette it anchors to the page's Due value, scrolling the least amount that shows it (the human asked, so 6.5 rule 2 is not broken); when the target is a row in a list rather than the open page, it anchors to that row. Top to bottom:

1. **Input**: the text input of 5, full width, 28. Starts empty and focused, placeholder "fri, in 3d, 15/12, 2026-12-15" (`due.picker.placeholder`). It parses on every keystroke with the grammar of 10.2.1, a pure function answered on the next frame.
2. 4, then the **preview line** (16, `type.small`): what `Enter` would set. Text that parses: the full date of 10.2.1 ("Friday 27 November 2026") in `text.primary`, then 8, the relative phrase coloured as in the Due row (`warning` for a past date). Text that does not parse: "Not a date. Try fri, in 3d, 15/12 or 2026-12-15." (`due.picker.invalid`) in `text.tertiary`; after `Enter` on it, the same words in `danger` with `triangle-alert` 14, and the input border `danger` (5, Text input, Invalid). A year outside 2000 to 2099: "Choose a date between 2000 and 2099." (`due.picker.range`), same two states. Empty input: the record's date in the same form, or "No due date" (`due.picker.none`) in `text.tertiary`.
3. 8, then the **month header** (28): month and year in `type.label` `text.primary` ("November 2026" / "Novembre 2026", 10.2.1), and at the right two compact ghost icon buttons 24, `chevron-left` and `chevron-right`, tooltips "Previous month" / "Next month" (`due.picker.prev`, `due.picker.next`) with their keys `PageUp` / `PageDown`.
4. 4, then the **weekday row** (16): the short weekdays of 10.2, Monday first in both languages ("Mon" to "Sun", "lun." to "dim."), `type.small` `text.tertiary`, centred on the columns.
5. 4, then the **grid**: 6 rows of 7 day cells, each 32 x 28, `radius.sm`, the day number `type.ui` with `tnum`, centred. Rest `text.primary`; days of the adjacent months `text.tertiary`. Hover `fill.hover`. **Today**: a 1px `border.strong` outline inside the cell, a shape that reads in greyscale (3.53 / 3.55 against `bg.overlay`). **Selected** (the date `Enter` would set: the parsed input, else the record's date): `fill.selected`, `type.ui-strong`, `text.primary` (8.08 / 11.37, 2.2). **Keyboard cursor**: the focus ring of 5. No colour on any cell; the accent is the focus ring only.
6. 8, then the **footer** (28), only when the record has a date: ghost button "Clear" (`common.clear`) at the left, a `Tab` stop. No chord clears from the input, because `Ctrl+Backspace`, `Alt+Backspace` and `Cmd+Backspace` already delete words or lines there.

Size: 248 x 300 (12 + 28 + 4 + 16 + 8 + 28 + 4 + 16 + 4 + 168 + 12), 336 with the footer; it fits above or below any anchor in the 880 x 560 minimum window.

Keyboard (focus stays in the picker until it closes, then returns to the anchor, 6.2):
- **Input**: typing parses. `Enter` sets the parsed date and closes; on an empty input it sets the selected day if it differs from the record's, else closes; on text that does not parse it shows the invalid state and sets nothing. `Down` moves the keyboard cursor into the grid, on the selected day, else today.
- **Grid**: `Left` / `Right` a day, `Up` / `Down` a week, `Up` from the first row back to the input; `Home` / `End` Monday / Sunday of the row; `PageUp` / `PageDown` a month, `Shift+PageUp` / `Shift+PageDown` a year, keeping the day number (clamped to the month's last day); `T` today; `Enter` or `Space` sets the cursor's day and closes. A cursor moved into another month turns the page to it.
- **Clearing**: `Delete` or `Backspace` in the grid, or the Clear button, clears the date and closes (when there is one). Outside the picker, `Delete` on the focused Due row and the palette command "Clear due date" do the same.
- **Anywhere in the picker**: `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` cycle input, grid, Clear; `Esc` closes and changes nothing.
- **Pointer**: a click on a day sets it and closes; the chevrons turn the month; a click outside closes and changes nothing.

**Writes.** Set, change and clear are optimistic, one `Mod+Z` step each, with no toast (the value visibly changes), and add a system event to the timeline (request 10): "Alexandre set the due date to 15 Dec 2026" (`event.due_set`), "Claude Code cleared the due date" (`event.due_cleared`); consecutive changes within 5 minutes collapse into the last one, as other events do. A write from Claude Code while the picker is open never changes what the human typed (6.5, rule 4): the remote value appears in the row behind, and a date the human then sets wins and is undoable as any edit.

**Topic table** (the collection on epic and project pages; the last section, under Knowledge). A toolbar row (28): the filter as a segmented control, Open / All / Closed (default Open), the count in `type.small` `text.tertiary` ("7 topics"), and at the right a ghost `plus` button, "New topic" (`Mod+T`). A header row (28, `type.small` `text.tertiary`, 1px `border.subtle` under it): Status (24, no label), Title (fills), Due (88, right-aligned, only while a topic of the page has a due date, below), Questions (64, right-aligned, only while a listed topic has an open question), Report to (120, left-aligned, only while a listed topic has a person; both in components-questions.md 5, Signals elsewhere), Entries (56, right-aligned), Updated (72, right-aligned, compact form of 10.2). Rows of 32, padding 0 x 8, `radius.sm`: status glyph 14, title `type.ui` `text.primary` truncated, the unread dot after the title, entry count and updated time in `type.small` `text.tertiary` with `tnum`. No lines between rows. Hover `fill.hover`; keyboard focus ring; the same keys as the tree (`Enter` open, `Mod+Enter` new tab, `S` status, `D` due date, `E` rename, `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down` reorder, `Delete`). Closed topics render as in the tree (glyph and title `text.tertiary`, 2.3).
- **Due column** (2026-11-21). It exists, with the two filter segments below, only while at least one topic the page lists, open or closed, has a due date: a person who never sets a date never sees either. The cell is the compact due form of 10.2.1, `type.small` with `tnum`, right-aligned in a 72 text box (the widest compact form, as for Updated). An overdue open topic adds `clock-alert` 12, 4 before the text, and draws icon and text in `warning`: icon and colour together, so it reads in greyscale. Due soon (today to 3 days ahead) is `text.secondary`; later dates `text.tertiary` (`text.secondary` on a selected row, 2.2); a closed topic's date is `text.tertiary` with no icon, whatever it is. Tooltip: the sentence form of 10.2.1 ("Overdue by 3 days, due 18 Nov 2026"). No date: an empty cell. Measured, `warning` on `bg.base`, on `fill.hover` and on `fill.selected` over it: 6.96 / 5.96 / 5.53 dark, 5.87 / 5.28 / 5.00 light.
- **Due filters.** With the Due column the filter reads Open / Overdue / Next 7 days / All / Closed (`table.filter.overdue`, `table.filter.week`). Overdue: open topics due before today. Next 7 days: open topics due before today plus 7, overdue included, because the question is "what must be done this week". A rolling seven days, not the calendar week, so that on a Sunday it still looks ahead. Both list by due date, earliest first, until a header is activated; under 10.3 the control becomes a dropdown when it does not fit. If the last date is cleared while one of them is selected, the filter falls back to Open. The count follows the filter ("2 topics").
- **Sort by Due**: ascending puts the earliest date first; topics with no date come last in both directions; equal dates keep the tree's order.
- Default order is the tree's order, the one the human arranged. Activating a column header sorts by it (`chevron-up` / `chevron-down` 12 after the label) and a ghost "Reset order" appears in the toolbar; while sorted, reordering by drag or key is off.
- On a project page the table is grouped by epic: group rows of 28 (`type.label`, the epic's name as a link, "3 of 7 done" in `type.small` `text.tertiary` at the right), topics outside any epic first under "No epic". `Enter` on a group row folds it.
- Under 640 of column width the Report to column is dropped (with every column shown, a 560 column leaves the title 136); under 520 the Entries and Questions columns go too; Due stays, since it is the column the Overdue filter is about. Empty: the existing empty states ("No topics yet. ..." with "New topic").
- It is a table and only a table. A board was considered and left out (Direction).
