# Acta design: questions and people

Part of the Acta design system. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) is the index: direction, principles, the never-list and the review checklist. Section numbers are shared across all parts.

## 5. Components (continued): questions and people

Added 2026-11-21 at the user's request: work often waits on an answer from someone else, and finished work often has to be reported back to someone. Both name a **person**, so both share one concept and one control. States, the focus ring and the controls used here (buttons, inputs, rows, menus, notice, empty state) are in [components.md](components.md); the date control is the due date picker of [components-records.md](components-records.md). Model and agent contract: data.md 11, requests 18 and 19. Copy: catalogue 10.4.6.

### Naming

| Concept | English | French | Why this word |
|---|---|---|---|
| The record | **Question** (section "Questions") | **Question** (section « Questions ») | The thing itself, the same word in both languages, short enough for a section header, a table column and the agent's `Q-n`. "Open questions" / "Questions en suspens" names a state, but the section also keeps answered ones; "Waiting on" / "En attente de" names the person, who is optional. Neutral about context: a question to a client, a landlord, a teacher or a city office reads the same. |
| Its states | Open · Answered · Dropped | En suspens · Répondue · Abandonnée | "Open" is the word the topic table already uses; French "En suspens" is the calm form of "open" for a question (Ouverte reads as "open-ended"). "Dropped" / "Abandonnée" as for topics. |
| Who is asked | **Asked** ("Asked Marie") | **Posée à** (« Posée à : Marie ») | A verb with a direct object in English needs no article; French takes the label-and-colon form so that free text ("le service RH") never meets a preposition ("à le"). |
| When the answer is expected | **Answer by** | **Réponse attendue le** | The due date language of 10.2.1: the same forms, the same "Overdue by 3 days". |
| Who to report back to | **Report to** ("Report to Marie") | **À rapporter à** (« À rapporter à : Marie ») | The user's own words ("à qui on devra les rapporter"). Not "assignee", not "owner", not "stakeholder": the person does none of the work and Acta assigns nothing. |
| The shared concept | **Person**, **people** | **Personne**, **personnes** | Plain; covers a person, a team or a place because the value is free text. Never "contact": there is no contacts system. |

### Person value

A person is **free text, not a record**: 1 to 80 characters, trimmed, inner whitespace collapsed, stored as typed ("Marie", "Legal team", "City hall, permits desk"). There is no people table, no address, no avatar and nothing to maintain.

- **Same person.** Two values are the same person when their **key** matches: case folded, accents removed, whitespace collapsed ("marie dubois" = "Marie  Dubois" = "Marie Dubois"). The key is used for suggestions, filters, the person page and the agent's `person` filter; it is never shown. Where one name is displayed for a key (suggestions, person page title, group headers), it is the spelling most recently written in the project.
- **Scope.** People are per project: the suggestions, the person page and the agent's filter only look inside the active project, as everything else does.
- **Where it is used.** A question's Asked; the Report to of a note, a topic and an epic. Not on projects (the user's decision): a project reports to no one, its topics and epics do.
- **Rendered.** Inside Acta's sentences in the label forms above, never re-cased or translated (10). In lists it is a link (`accent`, the link style of 5) to the person page, except inside a row that is itself the focus of the reader (a selected row: `text.primary` with underline on hover, 2.2).

### Person field

The one control that sets a person, used in four places: the question composer and editor, quick capture and the inbox capture editor, and the Report to property (as a popover). It is a **text input with suggestions**, not a picker of fixed values: typing a new name is the normal path.

- **Input**: the text input of 5, 28 high, placeholder "Person, team or place" (`person.placeholder`). Max 80 characters; at 80, further typing is ignored and the counter "80 / 80" (`type.small` `text.tertiary`, `tnum`) shows at the right inside the input.
- **Suggestions**: under the input, 4 below, a list of at most 6 rows. In a popover it is part of the popover; inline (composer, quick capture) it is a floating layer: `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.lg`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 4, the input's width, min 200. Rows 32, `radius.sm`, padding 0 x 8: the display name in `type.ui` `text.primary`, matched characters weight 600, and at the right in `type.small` `text.tertiary` `tnum` the person's open items in the project ("2 open", `person.open_count`: open questions asked to them plus things not yet reported to them), so "Marie" and "Marie D." are told apart by use. Hover `fill.hover`, highlighted row `fill.selected`; on either the count turns `text.secondary` (5.24 / 6.79 on `fill.hover`, 4.83 / 6.42 on `fill.selected`, over `bg.overlay`, dark / light, 2.2), because `text.tertiary` is not measured on a fill over `bg.overlay`.
- **Ranking** (a pure function, next frame, 10,000 records): with an empty input, the people with the most open items, then most recently used; with text, people whose key has a word starting with the typed key, then those containing it, each by open items then recency. A person whose key equals the typed key is listed first. Nothing is listed when no person matches.
- **Keys.** Typing filters. No row is highlighted until `Down` or `Up` is pressed, so `Enter` always takes **what was typed**; `Down` / `Up` move the highlight (and `Up` from the first row returns to the input text); `Enter` on a highlighted row takes that person's display spelling; `Tab` completes the input with the first (or highlighted) suggestion and stays in the field, then a second `Tab` moves on; `Esc` closes the list, a second `Esc` cancels the field's edit. `Enter` on an empty input clears the value where clearing is allowed. The pointer picks a row with a click.
- **Errors.** None to show: any text of 1 to 80 characters is a valid person.

### Questions section

On topic, epic and project pages, between Description and Knowledge (layout.md 4.2): on every record page the head of the page says what the work is and where it stands, the questions are its open edges, and the knowledge below is reference. Not a tab and not a side column, for the reasons the Knowledge section gives.

- **Header row** (28): "Questions" (`section.questions`) in `type.label` `text.secondary`; the count of **open** own questions in `type.small` `text.tertiary` (absent at 0); at the right a ghost `plus` button, tooltip "New question" with `Q`.
- **Open questions**, own ones only, in stored order (the order the human set: `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down`, drag; new ones go last). Never sorted by date or status, for the reason the tree gives (4.2.1): rows must not move under the reader. More than 6: the first 6, then a ghost row "Show all 14" (`common.show_all_n`) opening the Questions page scoped to this record.
- **"N closed" row** (ghost row, 28, `chevron-right` 14 then "3 closed" in `type.small` `text.tertiary`, `questions.closed`): answered and dropped questions, folded by default, the fold remembered per page (4.7, a record id and a boolean). Unfolded, newest closed first.
- **Roll-up** (epic and project pages only, last row): a ghost row "4 more open in the topics of this epic" (`questions.more_epic`) or "4 more open in this project's epics and topics" (`questions.more_project`), followed when some are overdue by ", 1 overdue" in `warning` as part of the same string (`questions.more_epic_overdue`, `questions.more_project_overdue`): one link, the overdue count coloured and worded so it reads in greyscale. It opens the Questions page scoped to the epic or the project. Absent at 0. A topic shows only its own questions: a question belongs where the answer unblocks work, and nothing is inherited downward.
- **Empty** (no question of any state): one line under the header in `type.reading` `text.tertiary`, "Nothing to ask yet. Note here what you are waiting to hear from someone." (`questions.empty`), with the roll-up row still below. Cost: 28 + 4 + 24.

**Question row** (padding 6 x 8, `radius.sm`, hangs 8 into the column gutter as list rows do; min height 32).
- **Leading**: glyph 14 in a 16 slot, top-aligned with the first text line: Open `circle-question-mark` (Lucide's current name of `circle-help`; new icon) in `text.secondary`; Answered `circle-check` in `text.tertiary`; Dropped `circle-slash` in `text.tertiary`. Shapes differ, so state reads in greyscale; no colour is spent on a question's state.
- **Text**, 8 after the slot: the question in `type.ui` `text.primary` (open) or `text.secondary` (closed). It **wraps**, never truncates: a question cut in half is a different question. At most 300 characters: five lines at most in a 480 column.
- **Answer** (answered questions with an answer text), 4 below the text: `type.reading` `text.primary`, left-aligned with the text, rendered Markdown, clamped to 6 lines with "Show all" (`common.show_all`).
- **Meta line**, 2 below the text, or 4 below the answer when there is one; `type.small` `text.tertiary`; items separated by " · ": Asked ("Asked Marie", `question.asked`, the name a link to the person page); the expected date as two runs, the label and absolute date "Answer by 4 Dec 2026" (`question.by`) then the relative phrase of 10.2.1 coloured exactly as in the Due row (`warning` overdue, `text.secondary` today to 3 days, `text.tertiary` later). An open question with neither a person nor a date has no meta line. On a closed question the date is not shown (an answered question is never overdue), and the line reads "Asked Marie · Answer recorded 3 Dec 2026 by Alexandre" (`question.answered_by`: who recorded it, since the person asked is the one who answered) or "Dropped 3 Dec 2026 by Claude Code" (`question.dropped_by`). When the answer was also logged as a decision, the meta line ends with "Logged as a decision" (`question.logged`), a link that opens the topic and focuses that entry.
- **Trailing**: the author mark 20 of the timeline (circle for Alexandre, square for Claude Code) for who **created** the question, top-aligned, then the unread dot (6, `accent`) left of it when another process created or changed the question since this installation last showed it (request 1). Same shape language as knowledge rows: kind leads, author trails.
- **States**: hover `fill.hover` and reveals, left of the author mark, ghost compact icon buttons 24: `check` "Mark answered" (`A`) on an open question, `ellipsis` (the menu). Keyboard focus: the focus ring around the row. Selected rows do not exist (no multi-select). Newly arrived from another process: the remote-change highlight of 6.5.
- **Row menu** (`Shift+F10`, the menu key, right click, `ellipsis`): open question: Mark answered `A`, Edit `E`, Set answer date... `D`, Clear answer date (when set), Drop question, Move to... `M`, Delete `Delete`. Closed question: Reopen, Edit answer `E` (answered), Delete.
- **Keys** on a focused row (the section is one stop of the page's `Up` / `Down`; `Enter` goes inside; `Up` / `Down` then move between rows; `Esc` leaves): `Enter` or `E` edits in place; `A` answers; `D` opens the due date picker for the answer date (the focused row wins over the page's own `D`, 5 Properties block); `Delete` (or `Cmd+Backspace`) deletes with the toast "Question deleted." and Undo; `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down` reorder open questions; `M` moves the question to another level of the same project (the palette lists the topic, its epic and the project first, then any epic or topic); `Q` adds a new question below the focused one.

**Composer** (adding). `Q` anywhere on the page (unless the focused element has its own meaning for the key), the header `plus`, or the palette's "New question" opens it as the last row of the open list, scrolled into view the least amount, focused:
1. A text input, full width, that wraps like the question text (`type.ui`, grows to four lines), placeholder "What do you need to know?" (`question.placeholder`). Max 300 characters; over 240 a counter "264 / 300" appears at its right.
2. 8 below, one line (28): the label "Asked" (`question.field.asked`, `type.small` `text.tertiary`), 8, the person field (240 wide; full width under 400 of column), then 16, the label "Answer by" (`question.field.by`), 8, a ghost button showing "None" (`due.none`) or the chosen date, which opens the due date picker anchored to it. Under 400 of column the date pair wraps to a second line.
3. Keys: `Enter` in the text saves (a question is one paragraph; `Shift+Enter` is not a newline, it does nothing); `Mod+Enter` saves from any field; `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` move text, Asked, Answer by. After saving, the composer stays open and empty, focused for the next question, so that five questions after a meeting are five `Enter`s; `Esc` closes it and keeps an unsaved draft (request 15). A question needs text: `Enter` on an empty composer closes it without writing.
4. Editing an existing question (`E`) uses the same three fields in place of the row; `Enter` or `Mod+Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels.

**Answering** (`A`, the `check` button, the menu, the palette's "Mark question answered"). The row stays where it is and grows an **answer editor** under its text: the multi-line editor of 5, `type.reading`, placeholder "What was the answer?" (`question.answer_placeholder`), focused; under it, 8, a row (28) with, on a question owned by a topic, the checkbox "Also log it as a decision" (`question.log_decision`), then at the right the ghost "Cancel" (`Esc`) and the primary "Save answer" (`question.save_answer`, `Mod+Enter`). An empty answer is allowed: the question is marked answered with no text ("answered elsewhere"). Saving moves the row to the "N closed" group on the next frame; the keyboard focus goes to the next open question, or to the section header when none is left.
- **The decision offer.** The checkbox exists only for questions owned by a topic, because only topics have a timeline. Its state is remembered per installation (a preference, a boolean, 4.7). Checked, saving also adds a **Decision** entry to the topic's timeline by the same author, whose body is the question as a Markdown quote line followed by the answer (`> Can we keep the v1 export until March?` then a blank line, then the answer): no words added by Acta, so nothing in it is in the interface language. Answer and entry are **one** `Mod+Z` step. The question keeps a link to the entry ("Logged as a decision").
- `Mark answered` on a closed question is absent; "Reopen" puts an answered or dropped question back at the end of the open list, keeping its answer text in the editor's draft should it be answered again.

**Writes.** Every change (create, edit, answer, drop, reopen, reorder, move, answer date, delete) is optimistic and one `Mod+Z` step, with no toast except delete. Questions write **no system events**: the question itself records who created, answered or dropped it and when, and the decision entry is the deliberate way to put an answer in the timeline. A question written by Claude Code counts as a write to its owner for the unread dot (data.md 11). A field being edited is never overwritten (6.5, rule 4).

### Report to

**Report to row** (properties block of topic and epic pages, after Due; components-records.md 5). Label "Report to" (`prop.report_to`). Like Due, the row exists only while a person is set (clarity review 2026-11-22).
- **Unset**: no row; the add chip "Report to" (`prop.add_report_to`, components-records.md 5, Properties block), `R` from anywhere on the page, or the palette opens the **person popover** anchored to the chip (or, with no pointer, to the line where the row will appear).
- **Set**: the person as a link to the person page, then 8, the report state as a run in `type.ui`:

| Case | Run | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Open record, not reported | none | |
| Topic Done, or epic at 100%, not reported | "Not reported yet" (`report.pending`) | `text.secondary` |
| Reported | "Reported 3 Dec 2026" (`report.done`) | `text.tertiary` |
| Topic Dropped | none (nothing is reported about dropped work unless the human marks it) | |

  On hover or focus of the row, a ghost compact icon button 24, `check`, "Mark reported" (`report.mark`), appears at the right of the run (on a reported row: `rotate-cw`, "Mark not reported", `report.unmark`). The row's menu (`Shift+F10`): Change person... `R`, Mark reported / Mark not reported, Go to {name}, Clear. `Delete` on the focused row clears.
- **Person popover**: a floating layer as the due date picker's (`bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.lg`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 12, width 248, 4 below the anchor, flips above): the person field with its suggestions inside the popover, then, when a person is set, 8, a footer (28) with the ghost "Clear" (`common.clear`). `Enter` sets and closes; `Esc` closes and changes nothing; focus returns to the row.
- **Reported** is a fact the human (or Claude Code, when asked) records; Acta never infers it. Changing the person resets "reported". Writes are one `Mod+Z` step each, no toast, and add a system event to the topic's timeline (request 10): "Alexandre noted to report to Marie" (`event.report_set`), "Alexandre marked it reported to Marie" (`event.reported`), "Claude Code removed Report to" (`event.report_cleared`).

**Notes.** A note can carry a Report to:
- **Capture** (quick capture, 6.1, and the inbox's capture editor): under the editor, one row (28): the label "Report to" (`type.small` `text.tertiary`), 8, the person field, inline. `Tab` from the note editor moves to it (in capture, `Tab` does not indent), `Shift+Tab` back; `Mod+Enter` saves from either. The field starts empty each time: a remembered person would silently tag the next, unrelated note. There is no `@name` parsing in the note text: notes hold e-mail addresses and handles, and Acta never rewrites what was typed.
- **Note card** (components-records.md 5): the meta row adds "Report to Marie" (`report.to`, the name a link) after the time. Filed notes keep it, in the same `text.tertiary`.
- **Filing carries it.** A note made into a topic or an epic ("Make topic", or Claude Code's triage) gives its person to the new record's Report to. Several notes into one record: the first note's person; if the notes name different people, the UI "Make topic" never meets the case (one note), and triage shows it in the plan for the human to settle (data.md 11, request 19). A note filed as an entry of an existing topic gives its person to that topic only when the topic has none; otherwise the topic keeps its own and the plan says so. A dismissed note's person goes nowhere.
- **Inbox filter**: while any listed note has a person, the toolbar gains, after the Open / Filed control, a compact secondary dropdown "Report to: anyone" (`report.filter.all`), menu "Anyone" (`report.filter.anyone`) then each person of the listed notes with its count; a non-default value reads in `text.primary` weight 600 ("Report to: Marie", `report.filter`), as the knowledge page's filters do.

### Questions page

A page (one per project, scoped to the project, an epic or a topic; a fixed page key with the scope's record id, so it is a tab like the knowledge base page). Reached from the roll-up rows, from "Show all 14", and from the palette ("Go to questions", the active project).
- Top to bottom in the content column: title "Questions" (`type.title`); 8, the scope line in `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "Acta · 7 open, 12 closed" (`questions.scope`, with `level.*` names when narrower: "Epic Terminal · 3 open, 5 closed"), and when the scope is below the project a ghost link "Whole project" (`questions.whole_project`); 24, the toolbar (28): a segmented control Open / Overdue / Closed / All (`table.filter.open`, `table.filter.overdue`, `table.filter.closed`, `table.filter.all`), Overdue present only while an open question in scope has a past answer date, as the topic table's due segments; default Open.
- The list is grouped by owner, widest first ("Project Acta", "Epic Terminal", "Topic Embed the terminal"; group headers of 28 as on the knowledge base page, the name a link, the count at the right). Rows are question rows and behave identically, except `Enter`, which opens the owner page with that question focused (a list page opens; a section edits); `E` edits in place here too. Overdue lists open questions whose answer date is before today, earliest first, ungrouped, each row's meta naming its owner ("in Topic Embed the terminal", `question.in`).
- Empty filter: "No open questions in Acta." (`questions.none_open`), "No overdue questions." and "No closed questions yet." in `type.reading` `text.secondary`, no action (the action lives on record pages).

### Person page

The answer to "what do I ask Marie, and what do I tell her?": everything in the project that names one person. A page; reached from any person link and from the palette ("Go to person...", which lists the project's people with their open counts and opens the chosen one).
- Title: the display name (`type.title`, user text, not translated); 8, the scope line in `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "In Acta · 2 questions to ask, 3 things to report" (`person.scope`, each count its own plural string joined by ", " as 10.3 licenses).
- **Questions asked** (`person.section.asked`): this person's open questions, as question rows with their owner in the meta line ("in Topic Embed the terminal", `question.in`), ordered overdue first by date, then by date, then undated in stored order (here the reader asks "what is late with Marie", and the rows are not the owner's list). Then the "N closed" row, folded.
- **To report** (`person.section.report`): the topics and epics whose Report to is this person, not marked reported and not Dropped, then the open notes that name them. Rows of 32 (padding 0 x 8): the leading status glyph (topic), `layers` (epic) or `sticky-note` (note), title (a note shows its first line) in `type.ui` `text.primary` truncated, then right-aligned in `type.small`: "Not reported yet" in `text.secondary` for closed work; for open work, a topic's status word, an epic's progress ("3 of 7 done", `table.group_progress`), a note's capture time in the compact form of 10.2, in `text.tertiary`. Ordered: not reported yet first (the work is done; telling them is what is left), then open work in tree order, then notes newest first. `Space` toggles reported on a topic or epic row (the `check` ghost button on hover does the same); `Enter` opens the record (a note opens the inbox at that card). Then "N reported" (`report.reported_count`), folded, newest first.
- Empty (the last item naming them was cleared while the page was open): "Nothing open with Marie." (`person.empty`) in `type.reading` `text.secondary`.
- A person page is a tab like any other while the app runs, but it is **not restored** after a restart: its key is a name, which is content, and `preferences.json` holds no content (4.7). The tab is dropped from the saved list; nothing else is lost.

### Signals elsewhere

- **Tree: none.** An open question waits on someone else and a Report to is a reminder, neither is "needs you now", and the tree's one trailing slot already orders unread, overdue and due today (4.2.1). A question mark or a person mark in the tree was considered and refused: it would be a third lasting mark competing for the slot and would mark rows for weeks. The questions of a topic surface on its page, in the topic table and in the briefing.
- **Topic table** (components-records.md 5): an optional **Questions** column (64, right-aligned, header "Questions"): the count of the topic's open questions, `type.small` `text.tertiary` `tnum`, empty at 0; when one of them is overdue the count is `text.secondary` and the tooltip says it ("2 open questions, 1 overdue", `table.questions_tip`); no icon, so it never reads as the Due column's overdue mark. An optional **Report to** column (120, left-aligned, header "Report to"): the person, `type.small` `text.secondary`, truncated with the tooltip; "Not reported yet" topics keep the name in `text.secondary`, reported ones `text.tertiary`. Each column exists only while at least one listed topic uses it.
- **Overview** (components-records.md 5): the fifth attention line, "2 questions past their answer date" (`overview.questions_late`, `circle-question-mark` in `warning`), on the project page and in the epic's "Needs attention"; it opens the Questions page with Overdue selected (`cmd.show_questions_late`). Report to adds no attention line: the Overview keeps five at most.
- **Status bar, titlebar, tabs**: nothing.
