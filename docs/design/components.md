# Acta design: controls and overlays

Part of the Acta design system. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) is the index: direction, principles and the never-list; the review checklist is [checklist.md](checklist.md). Section numbers are shared across all parts.

## 5. Components

**Revealed controls never move anything** (user feedback 2026-11-22: hovering the Folders row made the "Due date" chips appear under the properties and pushed the page down). A control that appears on hover or keyboard focus (add chips, a row's `ellipsis`, a ghost `plus`, a hover `x`, Show terminal on a session row, a fold chevron) takes space that is **already reserved at rest** or is **drawn over space that is empty at rest**; it never inserts a line, widens a row, shifts a label or pushes content. The audit of 2026-11-22 found one breach, the properties block's add chips, fixed in components-records.md 5 (they now sit in the 24 band under the block, which is empty at rest), and its copy on the contact page. Every other revealed control was already reserved: the tree's trailing slot (4.2.1), the tab's trailing slot (4.4), the note card's buttons in its meta row, the timeline entry's `ellipsis` over its header line's empty right end, the knowledge row's `ellipsis` over the time column, the folder row's active-mark slot, the contacts group `plus` and the session row's buttons at the row's right end, which is empty at rest, and the section fold chevron after the label. A new revealed control must name which of the two it uses.

States are listed as default / hover / pressed / focus / disabled unless noted. **Focus** always means: a 2px `focus.ring` band drawn just inside the control's edge, same radius, shown only for keyboard focus (not after a click); it takes no layout space and no clipping parent can cut it. On a primary button a 1px `bg.base` band sits inside the ring so it reads against the accent fill. Rows show focus the same way. Controls that own a focus handle and sit in open space (the theme and language controls of Settings) draw it 1px outside their edge instead, radius plus 1. (M7: GPUI paints shadows under a transparent control, so an outside ring on any control would need a wrapper around every button; the inside band keeps the ring visible everywhere.)

**Button** (height 28, padding 0 x 12, `radius.sm`, `type.ui`, optional 16 icon with gap 8; icon-only is 28 x 28, or 24 x 24 compact)

| Variant | Default | Hover | Pressed | Disabled | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | fill `accent`, text `text.on-accent`, `type.ui-strong` | `accent.hover` | `accent.pressed` | fill `fill.selected`, text `text.disabled` | At most one per view or dialog |
| Secondary | no fill, 1px `border.strong`, text `text.primary` | `fill.hover` | `fill.pressed` | border `border.default`, text `text.disabled` | Alternative actions |
| Ghost | no fill, no border, text `text.secondary` | `fill.hover`, text `text.primary` | `fill.pressed` | text `text.disabled` | Toolbars, rows, icon buttons |
| Destructive | fill `danger`, text `text.on-accent` | fill at 90% opacity | fill at 80% opacity | as primary | Only inside a confirmation dialog |

Destructive actions outside a dialog are ghost buttons or menu items with `danger` text. Buttons never change size between states and never show a spinner; a slow action disables the button and reports through a toast.

**Text input** (height 28, padding 0 x 8, `radius.sm`, fill `bg.raised`, 1px `border.strong`, `type.ui`, placeholder `text.tertiary`, caret `accent` 1.5px, no blink animation easing: on 530 ms / off 530 ms, solid while typing). Hover: unchanged. Focus: border becomes `focus.ring` at 2px (drawn inside, no layout shift). Invalid: border `danger`, message below in `type.small` `danger` with `triangle-alert` 14. Disabled: border `border.default`, text `text.disabled`.

**Inline title edit**: titles (topic, epic, project) are edited in place in their own type role with no box; a 1px `border.strong` underline appears while editing. `Enter` commits, `Esc` reverts.

**Multi-line editor** (description, current state, a timeline entry being edited, note capture): the live-preview Markdown editor of components-editor.md (the block with the caret shows its source, the rest renders) in `type.reading`, fill `bg.raised`, 1px `border.strong`, `radius.lg`, padding 12 x 16, minimum 3 lines (72), grows with content to 60% of the viewport height, then scrolls. Focus as text input. Footer row inside the box (28): left, context control (composer: entry type segmented control); right, hint `Mod+Enter to save` in `type.small` `text.tertiary` and the primary button. `Mod+Enter` commits, `Esc` cancels (if the draft is non-empty it is kept and restored next time; no "discard?" dialog). `Tab` indents when the caret is in a list item and moves focus to the next control otherwise. Read mode to edit mode: `Enter` or `E` on the focused block, or a click on its text; the block does not move.

**List / tree row** (height 28, padding 0 x 8, `radius.sm`, gap 8): leading slot 16, title `type.ui` `text.secondary` truncated at the end, trailing slot. Hover `fill.hover`, title `text.primary`; selected `fill.selected`, title `text.primary`; keyboard cursor: focus ring, cursor and selection separate until `Enter`. Used by the tree (below) and by the pinned Trash and Settings rows (icon 16 in the leading slot, no trailing slot).

**Tree row** (the rows of the Projects view; structure, order, geometry and signals in 4.2.1). Anatomy: indent (16 per depth), leading slot 16, 8, title, 8, trailing slot. The row is one hit target; the chevron is a second one inside it (a click on it folds, a click elsewhere opens).

| Row kind | Leading | Title role and rest colour | Trailing (one at most, 4.2.1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project | chevron 16 `text.secondary` | `type.ui-strong`; `text.primary` when it is the active project (4.6), else `text.secondary` | 24 reserved: dot when folded with unread inside, else its own due mark; `plus` on hover |
| Epic | chevron 16 `text.secondary` | `type.ui-strong`; `text.primary` when the open page is in it, else `text.secondary` | 24 reserved: dot when folded with unread inside, else its own due mark; `plus` on hover |
| Inbox, Knowledge | `inbox`, `library` 16 `text.secondary` | `type.ui` `text.secondary` | Inbox: count `type.small` `text.tertiary` `tnum`, above 0 only, "99+" above 99. Knowledge: dot |
| Topic, open | status glyph 14 in its colour (Draft `text.tertiary`, Planned `text.secondary`, Active `accent`, Blocked `warning`), centred in the 16 slot | `type.ui` `text.secondary` | dot, else due mark: overdue `clock-alert` 14 `text.secondary`, due today `clock` 14 `text.tertiary` (4.2.1) |
| Topic, closed | glyph 14 `text.tertiary` (Done, Dropped) | `type.ui` `text.tertiary`, no strikethrough | dot (a closed topic is never overdue) |
| "N closed" (ghost) | chevron 14 `text.tertiary`, centred in the 16 slot | `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "{n} closed" (`tree.closed`) | dot when folded with an unread closed topic |
| "New topic" (ghost) | `plus` 14 `text.tertiary` | `type.small` `text.tertiary`: "New topic" (`topic.new`) | none |

Two weights only, and weight marks structure, never state: projects and epics are 600, everything else 400, whatever its state. A selected topic stays at 400, so its title does not widen and re-truncate when it is selected, and it never reads as an epic.

States (every kind; "tertiary items" are the Draft and closed glyphs, closed titles, counts and ghost labels):

| State | Fill | Title | Leading | Trailing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rest | none | as in the table above | as above | as above |
| Hover | `fill.hover` | `text.primary` | chevrons and icons `text.primary`; status glyphs keep their colour | project and epic: `plus` replaces the dot (the dot returns when the pointer leaves); others unchanged |
| Pressed | `fill.pressed` | `text.primary` | as hover | as hover |
| Selected (the page open in the focused group's active tab, or a document's owner) | `fill.selected` | `text.primary`, weight unchanged | icons `text.primary`; Active and Blocked keep `accent` and `warning`; tertiary items become `text.secondary` (2.2 forbids `text.tertiary` on `fill.selected`) | count `text.secondary` |
| Keyboard cursor | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged | project and epic: unchanged (the `plus` is for the pointer; `Mod+T` and the palette are the key path) |
| Being dragged | the source row at 60% opacity | | | |
| Drop target (an epic or project row that would receive a topic) | `accent.subtle` | `text.primary` (2.2: nothing else on `accent.subtle`) | `text.primary` | hidden |
| Renaming (`E`, `F2`) | none | the title becomes an inline edit (as Inline title edit), the leading slot stays | | hidden |
| Disabled | none: no tree row is ever disabled | | | |

Measured on `bg.sunken` (2.2), dark / light: titles `text.secondary` 8.02 / 6.82 at rest, 6.41 / 5.81 selected; `text.primary` 13.42 / 12.08 at rest, 10.73 / 10.28 selected; `text.tertiary` 6.18 / 5.33 at rest, 5.36 / 4.79 hovered; Active glyph 7.26 / 5.33 at rest, 6.30 / 4.80 hovered, 5.81 / 4.54 selected; Blocked glyph 7.45 / 5.52 at rest, 6.46 / 4.96 hovered, 5.96 / 4.70 selected; unread dot 7.26 / 5.33; `text.primary` on `accent.subtle` 10.09 / 9.72. The step from a path row's `text.primary` to another project's `text.secondary` measures 1.67 / 1.77: enough to separate, too little to shout.

Behaviour:
- **Click** opens the row's page in the focused group's active tab and puts the cursor on the row. `Mod+Enter`, `Mod`+click or a middle click opens it in a new tab. A click on the chevron folds or unfolds and opens nothing. A ghost row is activated by a click, `Enter` or `Space`: "N closed" folds and unfolds, "New topic" opens the palette in create mode for that epic (as the `plus` does).
- **Hover `plus`** (project and epic rows only): compact ghost icon button 24, `plus` 16, tooltip "New topic" with `Mod+T`; it creates in that project (in no epic) or in that epic. No other row has hover controls; everything else is in the row menu (`Shift+F10`, right click).
- **Unread dot tooltip**: "New changes by Claude Code" (`tree.unread`), on a 16 x 16 hit box around the dot, after the usual 500 ms. **Due mark tooltip**: the sentence form of 10.2.1 ("Overdue by 3 days, due 18 Nov 2026", "Due today"), same hit box and delay.
- **`D`** on a focused project, epic or topic row opens the due date picker (components-records.md 5) anchored under the row's title; on any other row it does nothing.
- **Title tooltip**: only when the title is truncated (the shaped line is wider than its box), the full title, after 500 ms (5, Tooltip).
- **Drag to reorder** (the pointer path of `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down` and `M`). A press on a project, epic or topic row that moves 4 starts a drag; Inbox, Knowledge, ghost rows and the sticky row do not drag. The source row drops to 60% opacity and the drag chip of 4.8.7 follows the pointer (its icon is the row's leading glyph or icon at 14, its name the title, truncated at 240 of chip width). Targets:
  - a topic: between two topics of any group of its own project (a 2px `accent` insertion line, `radius.xs` ends, from the target depth's leading slot to the row's content edge); onto an epic row, folded or not, to move it to the end of that epic's topics (the row takes the drop-target state); onto its project row to move it out of its epic, to the end of the topics in no epic;
  - an epic: between epics of its own project (line at depth 1);
  - a project: between projects (line at depth 0, before or after a whole project block).
  Anything else is no target: nothing is drawn and the pointer shows the not-allowed cursor; moving a topic to another project is not a drag (it is not a move the model makes in one step). Within 28 of the viewport's top or bottom edge the tree auto-scrolls, up to 12 per frame, faster the nearer the edge. Dropping writes once, optimistically, as one `Mod+Z` step, with no toast (the row is visibly where it was dropped). `Esc`, a drop outside the tree, or a drop on the row's own place changes nothing. If another process deletes or moves the dragged record, the drag ends as cancelled.
- **Sticky project row** (4.2.1, long trees): a copy of the project row at the top of the tree viewport, same geometry and states, on `bg.sunken`, with a 1px `border.subtle` line across the viewport under it while it is stuck (rows pass under it, so space cannot separate them). It behaves as the project row: a click opens the project page; a click on its chevron folds the project and scrolls so the project row sits where the sticky row was. It is not a keyboard stop: the cursor moves through the real rows, and the tree scrolls so the cursor's row is never under it. Never more than one; it never moves with an animation, only with the scroll.
- **Remote changes** follow 6.5: rows Claude Code creates, moves or closes appear in place at once, never move the row under the pointer or the cursor, and never take focus.

**Status indicator**: glyph only (14, tree, tabs and tables) or glyph 16 plus word in `type.ui` (the Status property, menus). In a topic's properties it is a ghost button (height 28, padding 0 x 8) opening the status menu; shortcut `S`. The menu lists the six statuses in the table order with number hints 1 to 6; the current one has a trailing `check`.

**Segmented control** (height 28, 1px `border.strong` outline, since it is a control's only boundary, `radius.sm`, segments padding 0 x 12, `type.ui` `text.secondary`): selected segment `fill.selected`, `type.ui-strong`, `text.primary`; hover `fill.hover`; arrows move between segments when focused. Used for the timeline filter (All / Progress / Decisions / Discussion), the composer entry type, the inbox filter (Open / Filed), the topic table filter (Open / All / Closed) and the theme setting. It never switches pages; page tabs are a different thing (4.4).

**Slash menu** (in every multi-line editor and the document editor; the editor's other keys, the selection bubble and paste are in components-editor.md). Typing `/` at the start of a line or right after whitespace opens a menu at the caret: the context menu component, 8 rows visible and scrolling beyond that, filtered as typing continues by prefix of the item's name, never fuzzily ("/co" leaves Code block), `Enter` inserts, `Esc` closes and leaves the typed text alone. It must never get in the way of text that merely contains a slash, so: it does not open inside a code fence or inline code; it closes, leaving the text as typed, the moment what follows the `/` contains a second `/`, a `.`, a `\` or a space, or matches no item (so " /src/main.rs", " /usr" and " /etc" are just text); and once it has closed for a given `/` it does not reopen for it. With the menu closed, `Enter` and `Space` do what they always do. Every item inserts Markdown and nothing else: Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3, Bulleted list, Numbered list, Task list, Quote, Code block, Table (a 3 x 2 skeleton), Divider. The stored text is plain Markdown; there are no blocks, so what the menu writes is exactly what Claude Code would have written.

**Notice** (the one inline message component; used for edit conflicts, deleted-while-open, version headers, plaintext warnings, migration reports): full column width, `bg.raised`, 1px `border.subtle`, `radius.lg`, padding 6 x 12, a 28 first line shared by icon, message and actions (the icon stays on the first line when the message wraps; a one-line message is level with the ghost actions), optional icon 16 (`info` in `text.secondary`, `triangle-alert` in `warning` or `danger`), message in `type.ui` `text.primary`, ghost actions right-aligned. Never a coloured fill, never dismisses itself, never more than one per view.

**Checkbox** (export options, a folder's commit options and the terminal's save-folder option): 16 box, `radius.xs`, 1px `border.strong`, label `type.ui` 8 to its right; checked: fill `accent`, `check` 12 in `text.on-accent`; hover `fill.hover` inside the box; focus ring; disabled `border.default` and `text.disabled`.

**Command palette**: width 640 (or window width minus 48), top edge 96 below the window top, `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.xl`, `shadow.overlay`, no scrim (the user keeps seeing what they are acting on). Input row 44: `search` icon 16, text `type.reading`, optional scope chip (height 24, `accent.subtle`, `radius.sm`, `type.small` `text.primary`: "Topics", "Projects", "Status"). Results: up to 10 rows of 32, grouped under `type.label` headers in `text.tertiary` (Topics, Epics, Knowledge, Commands, Projects; an epic row shows `layers` and its project as the path; a knowledge row shows its kind icon, its title, and its summary in place of the path); row = icon or status glyph 16, title `type.ui`, path in `type.small` `text.tertiary` ("Acta / Terminal"), right-aligned shortcut hint. Matched characters are `text.primary` weight 600 on a `text.secondary` title. Selected row `fill.selected`; the first row is always selected; hover does not move the selection until the pointer actually moves. Footer 28, `border.default` top, hints in `type.small`: "Enter open", "Mod+Enter open in new tab", "Esc close". This is Acta's search everywhere: there is no second search box for navigation, and the scope chip (`Tab` on a row) is how it narrows to topics, knowledge, commands or projects. No results: one line, "Nothing matches "oauth". Enter creates a topic with this title." **A title equal to the query** (case and surrounding spaces aside) ranks first across groups: its group moves to the top and that row leads it and is selected, so `Enter` opens what was named and never creates a duplicate (lead decision after the QA of 2026-11-22, where "Core UI" created a topic beside the epic and "Acta" opened a memory before the project). A picker's input shows "Search" as placeholder: its scope chip already names it.

**Context menu / dropdown menu**: `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.lg`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 4, min width 200, max 320. Item 28 (changed 2026-11-22 from 32 with the label size: tree rows are 28 and are pointer targets too), padding 0 x 8, icon 16 optional (14 beside a 13 label), label `type.menu` 13 / 18 (typography.md 3.2; was `type.ui` 14, which the user found too big), shortcut hint right in `type.small`. Hover and keyboard highlight are the same single `fill.selected` row. Destructive item: `danger` text, last, after a 1px `border.default` separator with 4 above and below. Disabled: `text.disabled`. Type-ahead selects by first letters. Submenus only for "Move to epic".

**Tooltip**: `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.md`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 4 x 8, `type.small` `text.primary`, optional kbd hint after the label. Appears after 500 ms of hover or immediately on keyboard focus, 6 away from the target, never over it; once one has shown, neighbours show instantly until the pointer rests elsewhere for 1 s. One line, no wrapping, no tooltip repeating a visible label (a truncated label is not visible: the **title tooltip** of a truncated tree row gives the full title in `type.small`, at most 360 wide, wrapping between words). The other exception is the **path tooltip** (working folders): the full path in `type.code` at 12 / 16, at most 480 wide (or the window width minus 32), wrapping only after a separator.

**Toast**: bottom-left of the page area, 16 from the edges and above the bottom dock if one is open; never over a tool window (the chat's web view would cover it), width 360 max, `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.lg`, `shadow.overlay`, padding 8 x 12, `type.ui`. Optional leading icon 16 (`check` in `success`, `triangle-alert` in `danger` or `warning`), message, one ghost action ("Undo") with its kbd hint, then a ghost `x`. The layout toasts of 4.8.11 are the one case with two actions: "Undo" then "Reset layout". Stays 6 s (errors stay until dismissed), pauses while hovered or focused; at most one visible, a new one replaces the old. Toasts exist for undo and for failures only. With the page area split, "the page area" is the whole area, not one group.

**Dialog**: centred, width 440, `bg.overlay`, 1px `border.default`, `radius.xl`, `shadow.overlay`, over `scrim`. Padding 24; title `type.heading`; body `type.ui` `text.secondary`, 8 below the title; buttons right-aligned, 24 below the body, gap 8, the confirming action last (first on Windows, following the platform). Focus starts on the safe button; `Esc` cancels; `Enter` activates the focused button only. Focus is trapped inside.

**Empty state**: left-aligned at the top of the content column, never centred in the pane, never illustrated. Heading `type.heading`, one sentence in `type.reading` `text.secondary` (max 480 wide), then the one action that fixes it as a button with its kbd hint. See 8.5.

**Scrollbar**: overlay, 8 wide track with no fill, thumb 4 wide (8 when hovered or dragged), fully rounded, `border.strong` at 50% opacity (100% hovered), minimum length 32. Appears while scrolling or when the pointer is within 12 of the edge, hides 800 ms after. Never takes layout space.

**Resize handle**: the 1px `border.subtle` divider between panes with an invisible 8 hit area that lies over the line and the pane before it (the sidebar for its handle, the page for the docks'), so the pane after it, such as the chat's web view, never covers it. Hover or drag: the line becomes 2px `accent` and the cursor changes to the resize cursor (no hover delay in v1). Dragging resizes live, every frame, clamped to the pane limits; double-click restores the default. Dragging a side area or the bottom panel under half its view's minimum hides it (snap to hide, 4.8.6). Keyboard: palette commands "Grow focused area" and "Shrink focused area" (steps of 40). The same handle sits between groups (4.8.6).

**Keyboard shortcut hint (kbd)**: each key in its own box: height 18, min width 18, padding 0 x 4, `radius.xs`, 1px `border.default`, `type.small` with `tnum`, `text.tertiary`, gap 2 between keys (the sans, not the mono, which lacks the key glyphs). macOS shows glyphs (`⌘ ⇧ ⌥ ⌃ ↵`), Windows and Linux show words (`Ctrl`, `Shift`, `Alt`, `Enter`). Inside a primary button the hint has no border and uses `text.on-accent` at weight 400.

**Update indicator** (a status bar item, at the right, before the version). Hidden when up to date and while a download runs. It exists for the one path the updater window does not cover: the check or download was too slow at launch, the app opened, and the download finished in the background. **Ready**: 6 dot in `accent` plus "Update ready – restart to apply" in `type.small` `text.secondary`; activating it (or the palette command "Restart to update") restarts into the new version and restores the window, tabs and terminal sessions; left alone, the update applies at the next launch. **Just updated** (first launch on a new version): "Updated to 0.4.1" in `type.small` `text.tertiary` for that session, opening the release notes; no dot. **Failed** three launches in a row: `triangle-alert` 14 in `warning` plus "Update failed"; click shows the reason and a "Download manually" link. Never a dialog, never a toast, never a badge on anything else.
